The AM4 socket and B550 board allow a direct swap to a Ryzen 7 5800X3D later — the best gaming CPU for this platform — without changing anything else.
◈
1080p at 60–144Hz
The RX 6500 XT handles esports titles easily at 120fps and demanding games at 60fps. Performance matched to the 144Hz IPS monitor included in this build.
◉
IPS Display Quality
The LG UltraGear uses an IPS panel — 178° viewing angles, accurate colours, and no colour shift. A clear step above the TN screens common at this price point.
⚡
600W Headroom
The 600W PSU runs this build efficiently and has enough capacity for a future mid-range GPU upgrade — RTX 5060 or equivalent — without any other changes.
